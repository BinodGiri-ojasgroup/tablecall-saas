Three steps, and nobody waves across the room

  1. Open the table

    When guests sit down, staff open the table and get a 6-digit PIN that belongs to that table alone.

  2. Guests scan and tap

    The QR code on the table opens your menu. Guests type the PIN, then tap Call waiter, Request bill, Request water or Need assistance.

  3. Staff answer

    The request shows up on every staff screen at once, with a sound. One tap on Accept and the guest sees that someone is on the way.

Why a PIN? A photo of your QR code can't call your waiters from across town, and a PIN stops working when the table closes.

Questions owners ask

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The QR code opens a normal web page in the phone's browser.

Can guests order or pay?

No. {{brand}} shows your menu and sends service requests. Orders and payments stay with your staff and your existing till.

What if the internet drops?

Staff screens show “Reconnecting” and catch up by themselves when the connection returns. Guests see the same message on their phone.

Which devices can staff use?

Any phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser. Leave a tablet at the pass or sign in on your own phone.

Is each restaurant's data separate?

Yes. Every restaurant has its own menu, tables, team and history, and staff can only see their own.
